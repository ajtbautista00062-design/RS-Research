# Spatial architecture retained in V5

PRE-only segmentation and physical area settings are unchanged from V4. The original design is preserved in itsa_recovery_v4/SPATIAL_METHOD.md.

V5 changes region observation support to cap the minimum spatial count at actual membership, records heterogeneity as provisional by default, and flags MMU limitations without automatically deferring valid classes. Strict heterogeneity and historical MMU deferral remain optional. Strong contradictions, missing essential temporal support and invalid fits remain indeterminate.

Read OPERATIONAL_METHOD.md for the current decision hierarchy, uncertainty products, future diagnostics and scientific limitations. No simulation, raster processing or tests were executed for V5.
